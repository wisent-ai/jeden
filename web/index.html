01 Design contract

Powerful agents need
hard boundaries.

Jeden separates inference, policy, tools, and the run loop. Each part stays visible, inspectable, and under your control.

The model can reason freely. The harness decides what may actually happen.

01

Inference, routed

Choose the right model per task. Route through infrastructure you control instead of hard-wiring your workflow to one provider.

02

Policy, explicit

Approvals, path jails, hooks, and tool permissions are local contracts—not hidden behavior inside a hosted agent.

03

Execution, accountable

Every read, command, edit, artifact, and approval remains attached to a durable session you can inspect and resume.
